Buildability Review

Are your drawings ready to price?

Most extras on a job come from what the drawings did not say. We find those gaps before the builders do.

£495 for a set of up to 20 drawings. Larger sets are quoted before we start.

You sendThe drawings, the engineer’s drawings, and any specification or schedule of works.
Complete?Is there enough here for a builder to give a fixed price? What is missing, trade by trade.
Coordinated?Where the architect’s, the engineer’s and the services drawings disagree, or leave a gap between them.
Buildable?Details that will be slow or costly to build as drawn. Access, sequence and items with long lead times.
Cost riskWhat is likely to come back as an extra or a provisional sum.
You getA written list of points, each marked fix before tender, worth fixing or note. The questions to put to your designer. A call to go through it. Five working days.
What it is notNot a critique of the design, and not a check on planning, structure or Building Regulations. Those stay with your architect and engineer. We do not redesign.
