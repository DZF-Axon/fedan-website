About

A family practice that prices the job before it builds it.

Fedan is a family firm. Daniel prices the work and runs it. Helen leads the design. Our own trades, and subcontractors we have vetted, do the building.

Who you deal with

Daniel Zhirov Fedan

Founder · cost and delivery

I started in construction in 2019, as assistant to the project manager on a £35m job. I ran document control there and my first packages: internal doors, balustrades and ironmongery.

After that I moved into site management, then project management, for a London main contractor. The jobs were high-end private houses, private flats, and a building of studio flats with shared living space. Along the way I did the buying and the pricing, and wrote the procedures the jobs ran on.

One thing I always do. If you change your mind part way through, say a finish that is already ordered, or a wall after the floor is set out, I tell you what it will cost and what it does to the date. In writing, before you decide.

I price your job and I run it. I speak English, Hebrew and Russian.

Before Fedan

Building runs in the family.

Daniel’s father has been building in Israel for 25 years. Helen designs. This house in Ramat HaSharon is their work: she designed it and he built it, about ten years ago.

It is not a Fedan contract, and Fedan Ltd did not exist then. We show it because it is where the firm’s way of working comes from: the designer and the builder at the same table, and the cost decided with the design. Fedan is the next generation of that, in London.

How we build

We make the heavy part light.

A project is heavy: money, decisions, trades. We carry that weight so you do not have to.

  • Our own tradesThe core of the work is done by trades who work with us directly, so we decide who is on your job and when.
  • A vetted network of subcontractorsFor specialist work we bring in subcontractors we have checked and used before. We know their work, their prices and how they perform on site. That is how we take on anything from one bathroom to a full office fit-out.
  • One contract, one point of contactYou deal with us. We appoint and manage everyone else.
  • Prices from real quotesEvery price is built from what the trades and suppliers actually charge, with our margin on top.
  • Clients stay privateWe never name a client or show their property without their say-so.
