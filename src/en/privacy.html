What we collect

What you type into the enquiry form or the Join the network form: your name, how to reach you, where the property is and what you tell us about the project or your work. If you email us, we keep the email.

To reply to you, to arrange a call or a site visit, and to assess applications to join our network. We rely on our legitimate interest in answering you, and on the steps you ask us to take before any contract.

Our team, and the companies that host this website and our email, only so the message reaches us. We do not sell your details or use them for marketing. Enquiries sent through the forms are processed and stored by Netlify, the company that hosts this website.

How long we keep it

As long as we need it to deal with your enquiry or application, and for any project that follows. Then we delete it.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details, or object to how we use them. Email info@fedangroup.com. If you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. The fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when a page loads.
